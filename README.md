# Verifact AI

> An Explainable AI Platform for Misinformation Detection and Claim Verification

## Overview

VeriFact AI is an AI-powered web platform designed to help users critically evaluate online information. Instead of declaring whether content is "true" or "false," the platform provides transparent analysis, identifies potentially misleading language, extracts factual claims, summarizes articles, and explains why certain content may require further verification.

The goal is to promote responsible digital literacy through explainable artificial intelligence.

---

## Features (Planned)

- Analyze pasted news articles or text
- Detect clickbait and sensational language
- Extract factual claims
- Highlight statements requiring verification
- AI-generated article summaries
- Explainable AI reasoning
- Confidence score with limitations
- Support for multilingual expansion
- Secure user authentication
- User dashboard and analysis history

## Ethical AI Statement

VeriFact AI is designed to assist users in evaluating information. It does not determine absolute truth and should not replace professional journalism, expert fact-checking, or human judgment.
